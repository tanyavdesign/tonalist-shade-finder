Get close enough that your face fills the frame — the screen is your only light source, and it's weak at arm's length. Turn your head about 45° to the right and include your neck in frame. Find even, indirect light and turn your screen brightness to maximum.

The reading uses eight brief full-screen colour flashes, about six seconds in total, to measure how your camera and screen respond before it measures your skin. The screen changes colour slowly rather than strobing. If flashing light is a problem for you, don't run it.

Set your device's own screen brightness to maximum before starting — there is no way for a web page to do this for you, so it has to be done in your phone's system settings first. Starting the camera does keep the screen from dimming or locking mid-sequence.
